Binance par PKR se Crypto Kaise Khareedein

Pehle yeh samajh lein

  • Binance duniya ka sab se bara crypto exchange hai, aur usay Pakistan ke regulator PVARA se No-Objection Certificate (NOC) mil chuka hai — matlab woh licensing ke amal mein hai.
  • Pakistan mein aap seedha bank card se crypto nahi khareed sakte. Aam tareeqa P2P (peer-to-peer) hai — aap kisi doosre user se PKR mein USDT khareedte hain, bank transfer ya Easypaisa/JazzCash ke zariye.
  • USDT ek "stablecoin" hai — iski qeemat hamesha taqreeban 1 US dollar rehti hai. Beginners ke liye sab se mehfooz shuruwat hai.
  • Sirf utne paise lagayein jitne ka nuqsaan bardasht kar saken. Yeh mashwara nahi, usool hai.

Step 1 — Account banayein

  1. Is link se Binance par jayein aur "Sign Up" par click karein. (Affiliate link — aapko koi extra charges nahi lagte.)
  2. Apna email address likhein aur mazboot password banayein (kam az kam 12 characters, numbers aur symbols ke saath).
  3. Email mein aaye verification code ko enter karein.
  4. Two-factor authentication (2FA) zaroor lagayein — Google Authenticator app use karein, sirf SMS par bharosa na karein. Yeh aapke account ki sab se bari hifazat hai.

Step 2 — KYC verification

P2P trading ke liye identity verification (KYC) lazmi hai. Ghabrayein nahi — yeh har regulated exchange ka rule hai:

  1. Profile mein jaakar "Verification" par click karein.
  2. Apna CNIC (front aur back) ki tasveer upload karein.
  3. Selfie verification mukammal karein — camera ke saamne chehra dikhana hota hai.
  4. Aam tor par kuch ghanton mein approve ho jata hai, kabhi kabhi 1–2 din lag sakte hain.

Step 3 — P2P se USDT khareedein

  1. Binance app mein "Trade" → "P2P" par jayein.
  2. Upar "Buy" select karein, coin mein USDT chunein, aur currency mein PKR.
  3. Payment method filter lagayein — Bank Transfer, Easypaisa ya JazzCash, jo aapke paas ho.
  4. Seller chunte waqt do cheezein dekhein: (1) completion rate 95% se zyada ho, (2) kam az kam 500+ orders mukammal kiye hon.
  5. Amount likhein (masalan Rs 5,000) aur "Buy USDT" dabayein.
  6. Seller ki di hui details par bank/Easypaisa se payment karein — Binance ke bahar koi payment na karein, aur order page par diye gaye account par hi bhejein.
  7. Payment ke baad "Transferred, notify seller" dabayein. Seller USDT release karega — aapke Funding Wallet mein aa jayegi.
  8. Agar seller coins release na kare to "Appeal" karein — Binance ki team payment ka saboot dekh kar faisla karti hai. Isi liye payment ka screenshot hamesha rakhein.

Step 4 — Apne coins mehfooz rakhein

  • Chhoti raqam exchange par rakhna theek hai, lekin bari raqam ke liye apna wallet (jaise Trust Wallet) use karein — "not your keys, not your coins."
  • Apne wallet ka recovery phrase kagaz par likh kar mehfooz jagah rakhein. Kabhi kisi ko na batayein — na dost ko, na "support" ke naam par message karne wale ko.
  • Binance ki taraf se aane wale jaali emails aur messages se hoshiyar rahein. Binance kabhi aapka password ya recovery phrase nahi maangta.

Aam ghaltiyan jo naye log karte hain

  • 2FA lagaye baghair bari raqam rakhna — hacked accounts ki sab se bari wajah.
  • P2P mein order cancel kiye baghair dobara payment kar dena.
  • Telegram/WhatsApp par "saste USDT" bechne walon par bharosa — 99% scam hota hai. Sirf Binance ke andar P2P use karein.
  • Pehele din hi leverage/futures trading shuru kar dena. Spot se shuru karein, futures ko bhool jayein jab tak samajh na aaye.
  • Apne tax records nahi rakhna — har khareed ki tareekh, raqam aur saboot save karein. Hamari tax guide parhein.

Mubarak! Aapne apni pehli crypto khareedne ka tareeqa seekh liya. Agla qadam: Crypto Tax Guide parhein taake qanooni tor par mehfooz rahein.
