How we rank exchanges

  1. PVARA compliance (most important): Is the exchange licensed or holding an NOC? Unlicensed platforms now face shutdown risk under the Virtual Assets Act 2026.
  2. PKR access: P2P depth, payment methods (bank, Easypaisa, JazzCash), and spreads.
  3. Fees: Spot trading fees and P2P spreads — small percentages compound.
  4. Security track record: Proof of reserves, hack history, and account protection (2FA, withdrawal allowlists).
  5. Beginner-friendliness: Clear interface, Roman Urdu / Urdu community support, responsive help.

Red flags — avoid any platform that…

  • Guarantees profits or "double your money" returns
  • Asks you to deposit to a personal wallet address outside the platform
  • Has no verifiable company behind it
  • Pressures you with countdown timers and "limited slots"
